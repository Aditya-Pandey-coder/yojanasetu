# YojanaSetu

### Your Bridge from Eligibility to Action

An AI-powered platform that helps citizens discover relevant government welfare schemes, understand eligibility, and receive a clear roadmap for what to do next.

<br/>

</div>

---

## The Problem

India has a vast ecosystem of government welfare schemes, yet many eligible citizens struggle to access the benefits available to them.

The challenge is not simply the availability of schemes.

Citizens often struggle with:

- Discovering schemes relevant to their situation
- Understanding complex eligibility criteria
- Knowing whether they actually qualify
- Identifying required documents
- Understanding what to do next

> **The real gap lies between information and action.**

---

## The Solution

**YojanaSetu** is an intelligent eligibility-to-action platform designed to simplify how citizens navigate government welfare schemes.

Instead of searching across multiple portals and interpreting complex criteria, users can describe their situation naturally and receive personalized guidance.

Citizen Input
      ↓
AI Understanding
      ↓
Eligibility Verification
      ↓
Explainable Recommendations
